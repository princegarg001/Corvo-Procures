Procurement Policy Amendment: proprietary OEM spares

Effective from 01-11-2026.

Spares that are proprietary to an OEM, namely items whose description names Vertex or Hydrax in brackets, such as "(Vertex CNC)", "(Vertex)" or "(Hydrax)", can only be sourced from the OEM channel. Where no rate contract applies and the item has been bought from an Active supplier in the last year, such items shall be ordered through a new route PROPRIETARY_PO with the 1st preferred supplier at its reference price, whatever the value.
