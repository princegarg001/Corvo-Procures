Policy Amendment: Emergency purchases at Chakan

With effect from 1 November 2026, the following exception is added to clause 5.3.

For the Chakan plant only, an emergency requirement valued up to ₹7.5 lakh may be placed as an Emergency PO with the 1st preferred supplier at its reference price, provided the item was bought from an Active supplier in the last 365 days.
Emergency requirements at the Bhosari plant continue to follow the ₹5,00,000 limit in clause 5.3.
