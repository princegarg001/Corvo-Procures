Corvo Engineering Pvt Ltd: Procurement Policy Amendment No. 1 to CORVO-PROC-POL-04

Subject: Revision of the Spot PO limit

Effective date: 10 November 2026

In view of the rise in input prices, the Head of Procurement has approved the following change.
The Spot PO limit in clause 5.4 is raised from ₹2,00,000 to ₹3,00,000.
All other provisions of the policy remain unchanged.
