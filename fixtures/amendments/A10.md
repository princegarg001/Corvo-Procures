Amendment: short lead-time requirements

With effect from 1 November 2026, a requirement whose need-by date is 3 days or less after the requirement date shall be treated like an emergency: if its value is within the emergency limit of clause 5.3 and the item was bought from an Active supplier in the last year, it may be placed as an Emergency PO with the 1st preferred supplier at its reference price. This check is applied before clause 5.3.
